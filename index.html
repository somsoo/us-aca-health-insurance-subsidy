How the ACA Subsidy Works

1

Determine FPL Tier

Your income is compared to the Federal Poverty Guidelines to find your exact FPL percentage.

2

Cap Contribution

Federal law caps your health insurance cost to between 0% and 8.5% of your household income.

3

Apply Tax Credit

The difference between the benchmark plan cost and your capped contribution is paid as a subsidy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Affordable Care Act (ACA) Premium Tax Credit?

The ACA Premium Tax Credit is a federal subsidy that lowers monthly health insurance premiums for eligible individuals and families purchasing coverage through HealthCare.gov or state exchanges.

What income qualifies for health insurance subsidies under the ACA?

Subsidies are available starting at 100% of the Federal Poverty Level. Under enhanced rules, premium contributions are capped at 8.5% of household income even for incomes above 400% FPL.

What are Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSR) and how do they work?

Cost-Sharing Reductions are extra discounts that lower out-of-pocket healthcare costs such as deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance when you enroll in a Silver category plan with income up to 250% FPL.
